Our Story

Our story

InnovateHER is a girls-led community dedicated to empowering young women nationwide to lead, create, and innovate with confidence. We believe every girl deserves access to opportunity, mentorship, and a space where her voice is valued and heard.

Through workshops, collaborative events, leadership initiatives, and meaningful conversations, we're building more than just a program — we're building a movement. A movement where girls support girls, ideas turn into action, and leadership starts now.

Whether you're passionate about STEM, business, advocacy, public health, the arts — or you're still figuring it out — you belong here. At InnovateHER, we don't just talk about the future. We build it. Together.
